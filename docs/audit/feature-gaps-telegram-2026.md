# Feature gaps against Telegram, April 2025 to August 2026

Status: living document, started 2026-11-10. Sources: Telegram's blog posts of the period (linked in each row) and
the `td_api.tl` of the pinned TDLib (a94b83d, TDLib 1.8.68), which already has every function the rows name. Each
feature was compared with Pingogram's `main` at fe6cac5. The owner's design for the features Pingogram builds is the
canvas "Pingogram — New Telegram features · M3 Expressive"; the board numbers below are its artboards.

The sessions building them, as the canvas groups them:

- **A**: messages and the chat list (`feature:chat`, `feature:chats`, and what they need in `core:domain` and
  `core:data`), boards 1–8.
- **B**: calls, stories and media (`feature:calls`, `feature:media`, the profile's music bar), boards 9–14.
- **C**: profiles, contacts, groups and settings (`feature:profile`, `feature:contacts`, `feature:settings`, the
  sign-in's passkey step), boards 15–22.

Each session ticks the box of a row as it ships it, in the pull request that ships it. A feature that needs a decision
the owner has not made is marked "Owner decision to confirm (2026-11-10)" in `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` and listed in the
pull request.

The priorities:

- **High**: everyday messaging, or what shows today as "Unsupported message" or a blank; every feature with a board.
- **Medium**: useful polish, no board, each a small pull request.
- **Large, waiting on the owner**: fits Pingogram's rules, but needs a decision (a new composer, a third party, a
  design the canvas does not show) before anyone builds it.
- **Left out**: contradicts `REQUIREMENTS.md` §1.2, `CLAUDE.md` rule 3 or rule 10, or belongs to another platform.
- **Already have**: Pingogram does it, or the feature is Telegram's servers' or a bot's and needs nothing in a client.

53 features in all: 25 high, 13 medium, 5 large, 5 left out, 5 already there.
