# Child safety standards

Kyra Labs, which makes Pingogram, has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation.

## Zero tolerance

Pingogram prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Using Pingogram
also means using Telegram, whose [Terms of Service](https://telegram.org/tos) prohibit them too.

## Where content lives

Pingogram is an app for Telegram accounts. It has no servers of its own: messages, photos, videos, groups and channels
are stored and moderated by Telegram. Telegram explains how it finds and removes child sexual abuse material on its
[safety page](https://telegram.org/safety).

## Report it in the app

- **A message**: press and hold it, choose *Report*, then choose child abuse among the reasons Telegram offers. To
  report several messages at once, select them and tap *Report* in the toolbar.
- **A person or a bot**: open their profile and choose *Report* under "More". If Telegram needs messages to go with
  the report, the app asks you to select them in your chat and report them there.
- **A group or a channel**: report its messages, as above.

Reports go to Telegram's moderators. Kyra Labs does not see them.

You can also report to Telegram directly: Telegram's [safety page](https://telegram.org/safety) names
<abuse@telegram.org> and <stopCA@telegram.org> for reports of illegal content and child abuse. Include links to the
content (`t.me/…`).

## Report it to us

Email **[contact@kyralabs.dev](mailto:contact@kyralabs.dev)** and say where you saw it: a `t.me` link, a username or
the name of the group or channel. Please send links and names only, never the images or videos themselves.

What we do with a report:

- We read this address and answer within a few days.
- We forward the report to Telegram's moderators.
- We report child sexual abuse material to the authorities: the [CyberTipline](https://report.cybertip.org/) of the
  National Center for Missing & Exploited Children, and the relevant national authority or hotline (the
  [INHOPE](https://www.inhope.org/) network lists them).
- Beyond that, we don't keep a report or share it with anyone.

If a child is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

## Compliance

Pingogram complies with applicable child safety laws and cooperates with law enforcement. Kyra Labs' designated
contact for these standards is [contact@kyralabs.dev](mailto:contact@kyralabs.dev).
