# Code of Conduct

Pingogram is a welcoming project. Everyone participating — in issues, pull requests,
discussions, and chat — agrees to:

- Be respectful and considerate; disagree on ideas, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and people of every background, identity and skill level.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive; no harassment, hate, trolling or personal attacks.
- Respect privacy: never share others' personal data, screenshots of private chats, or
  account details.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and comments that do not follow these
rules, and may temporarily or permanently ban participants for repeated or serious violations.

To report a problem, email the maintainer listed in `SECURITY.md` or contact them through a
GitHub private message. Reports are handled confidentially.
