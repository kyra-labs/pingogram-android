The first stable release. Group calls by link: add people from the call, turn a call into a group call, and join from the invitations in chats and Recent calls. A weekly clean-up of downloaded media not opened for 30 days, while the phone charges and is idle, keeps the cache under 4 GB; pinned chats keep their files. Closing a photo or video no longer stutters, the search field and filters keep their background, and contacts stay clear of the A–Z index.
