A demo for the app stores' reviewers, who cannot be given a Telegram account: pressing and holding the logo on the first screen opens sample chats stored on the phone, without an account or the network. Every screen works in it with sample data, calls included. A banner says so on every screen, and "Leave demo" in Settings deletes everything it made. Signing in with Telegram's test accounts is removed: Telegram's test servers no longer sign them in.
